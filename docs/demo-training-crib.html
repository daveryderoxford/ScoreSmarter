ScoreSmarter demo – training sheet crib

What to hand-write on the club scoring sheets for the three daily training races in the demo club (demo.ro.scoresmarter.app, Training season). Boats and entries are recreated every night from scripts/demo-training-sheet.json; keep this crib in step if that file changes.

  • Use the normal club sheet with the typewritten row numbers in the first column.
  • Write in the order shown. Rows marked  yellow  are deliberate mistakes so trainees see “Needs attention” flags and alternative readings.
  • Photograph the finished sheets once, then print the photos (or the sheets) for trainees to capture.
