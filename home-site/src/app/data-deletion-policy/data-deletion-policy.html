Data Deletion

Effective date: 16 October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of personal data associated with your ScoreSmarter account, including accounts created by signing in with Facebook or Google. It is intended to meet Meta’s requirements for Data Deletion Instructions.

How to request deletion

There is currently no in-app self-serve delete button. To request deletion, email support@scoresmarter.app with:

  • Subject line: Data deletion request
  • The email address you use to sign in to ScoreSmarter
  • Whether you signed in with Facebook, Google, or another method
  • Any club names associated with your account, if you know them

What we delete

  • Your Firebase Authentication account and sign-in linkage (including Facebook login association)
  • User profile and access records we hold for your account
  • Other personal data we control that is not required for club historical records, where practicable

Race results and related club racing records may be retained by the club as part of their sporting history (for example published series standings that name competitors). Removing your login does not automatically rewrite historical published results held for a club.

Timeline

We aim to acknowledge your request within a few business days and to complete deletion of account access and associated personal data we control within 30 days where practicable.

Facebook Apps and Websites

You can also remove ScoreSmarter’s access to your Facebook account in Facebook’s settings under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. That stops Facebook login for ScoreSmarter; you should still email us if you want ScoreSmarter to delete the account data we hold.

Contact

Email support@scoresmarter.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
