Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

ScoreSmarter provides software for sailing clubs to manage race entry, scoring, and published results. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. It applies to our websites (including scoresmarter.app), club applications, and related services (together, the “Service”).

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@scoresmarter.app.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or Facebook through Firebase Authentication, we receive identifiers such as your name and email address from that provider.
  • Club and racing data. Clubs and race officers enter information such as member and boat details, race entries, duties, and results. That data is stored for the club that uses the Service.
  • Technical information. We use Google Firebase services (including App Check) to operate and protect the Service. This may involve device or session-related signals needed for security and reliability.

How we use information

  • Authenticate users and manage access for clubs, race officers, and administrators
  • Provide race entry, scoring, duties, and published results features
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service
  • Respond to support and data requests

Sign-in providers

Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication on auth.scoresmarter.app. You may choose Google or Facebook as your identity provider. We do not receive your Facebook or Google password. Those providers process your login according to their own policies.

Service providers and hosting

We host and process data using Google Firebase, including Authentication, Cloud Firestore, App Check, and App Hosting. These providers act as processors that help us deliver the Service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run the Service (for example with Firebase), when a club publishes race results that include competitor details, or when required by law.

Retention

Account information is kept while your account remains active. Club racing records are retained so clubs can manage and publish historical results. You can ask us to delete your account data as described in our Data Deletion Policy.

Security

We use industry-standard controls provided by Firebase and role-based access within each club. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please use a strong account with your chosen sign-in provider.

Children

The Service is aimed at sailing clubs and race officers. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child under 13, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact support@scoresmarter.app to make a request. For account deletion instructions, see the Data Deletion Policy.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
